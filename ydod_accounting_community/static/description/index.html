Comprehensive Financial Management

All-in-One Accounting Suite for Odoo Community

Designed specifically for CFOs, accountants, and auditors who need complete accounting and reporting power without recurring enterprise license overheads.

Financial Statements

Printable QWeb PDF and Excel-compatible exports for Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss with comparative periods, Trial Balance, and Cash Flow.

Aged Receivables & Payables

Detailed partner aging breakdown across Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-120, and 120+ days to streamline debt collection and cash flow monitoring.

Fixed Assets & Depreciation

Automatic straight-line depreciation board scheduling, asset residual tracking, and one-click posting of periodic depreciation journal moves.

Analytic Budget Control

Set financial targets per analytic account, aggregate actual expenditures in real time, and monitor achievement percentages with visual progress indicators.

Smart Bank Recon & Matching

Reconciliation statement audit comparing bank balances to general ledger books, uncleared deposits/checks, with smart pattern-matching suggestions.

Multi-Level Approvals

Formal submit, approve, and reject workflows for journal entries and billing documents, ensuring strong internal controls and segregation of duties.

Automated Fiscal Closing

One-click year-end closing that clears all P&L accounts to Retained Earnings, locks prior periods, and prevents backward tampering.

Automated Payment Follow-Up

Dispatch email reminders for overdue receivables directly to customer contacts and automatically schedule follow-up activities.

Multi-Company & Multi-Currency

Complete data isolation across corporate entities, multi-currency ledger support, and granular Accountant and Manager security roles.

How to Use yDoD Accounting Community

1
Configure Fiscal Year

Open Accounting > Configuration > Fiscal Years to establish period boundaries and designated retained earnings accounts.

2
Manage Assets & Budgets

Create fixed assets to calculate depreciation schedules, and configure analytic budgets to monitor operational expenses.

3
Reconcile & Follow-up

Audit bank statements, auto-match counterpart journal lines, and dispatch automated payment reminder notifications.

4
Financial Reporting

Generate Balance Sheet, P&L with comparison, Trial Balance, and Cash Flow in ready-to-print PDF or Excel formats.
