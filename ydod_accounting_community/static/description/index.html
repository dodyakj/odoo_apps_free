Key Capabilities for Modern Finance Teams

Everything you need to operate a disciplined accounting practice without expensive enterprise tier upgrades.

Full Accounting Menu Suite

Unlocks standard Journal Entries, Journal Items, and configuration menus directly on the top navigation bar in Odoo Community.

Fiscal Years & Period Control

Define fiscal years with validated start/end boundaries, unique codes, and company isolation to guard period integrity.

Automated Year-End Closing

Zero out Income and Expense account balances into Retained Earnings with one click, generating a balanced closing journal entry.

Bank Reconciliation Audit

Print-Ready Audit Reports

Workflow Guide

How to Use in 5 Simple Steps

1
Setup Fiscal Year

Go to Configuration > Fiscal Years, define your dates and click Open Period.

2
Post Entries

Post invoices, bills, and adjustments using unlocked Journal Entries menus.

3
Reconcile Items

Open Manual Reconciliation to match open debtor and creditor counterpart lines.

4
Audit Bank Books

Run Bank Reconciliation Statement to verify bank ending balance against GL books.

5
Close Fiscal Year

Click Generate Closing Entry on the fiscal year to clear P&L to Retained Earnings.
