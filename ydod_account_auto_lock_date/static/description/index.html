Enterprise-Grade Financial Governance

Why Finance Teams Need This Module

Eliminate accidental postings in closed fiscal periods while preserving management flexibility for late adjustments.

Scheduled Auto-Lock

Automatically lock accounting dates to the last day of the previous month on your company's closing day (e.g., 10th of every month) via a reliable, automated daily cron.

Role-Based Lock Bypass

Chief Accountants and Financial Controllers in the dedicated bypass group can post, validate, and edit entries in closed periods without re-opening the books for standard users.

Full Audit Trail & Log

Every automated schedule execution, manual date change, and bypass posting is recorded with exact timestamp, previous date, new date, user, and reason for auditor compliance.

Flexible Lock Scopes

Choose which lock levels to advance automatically: Sales Entries, Purchase Entries, Tax Return Lock Date, Global Fiscal Year Lock Date, or tailor custom combinations.

Real-Time Chatter Tracking

Lock updates and bypass operations post formatted summary notes directly into the Company and Journal Entry chatter, ensuring complete operational transparency.

One-Click Manual Run

Need to close right away before the scheduled cron? Use the "Run Auto-Lock Now" button in settings or on the company form to execute the closing logic instantly.

Simple Setup

How It Works in 4 Simple Steps

1
Configure In Settings

Go to Accounting > Configuration > Settings and enable Scheduled Auto-Lock.

2
Set Closing Day

Choose your monthly closing day (e.g. 10th) and select which lock dates should advance.

3
Assign Bypass Roles

Add Chief Accountants or Controllers to the "Bypass Accounting Lock Dates" security group.

4
Review Audit Trail

Inspect every lock date change and bypass action under Accounting > Review > Lock Date Audit Trail.
