The Problem with Ad-Hoc Company Emails

In standard Odoo Community, there is no centralized announcement bulletin for HR teams. Important corporate policies, compliance warnings, holiday greetings, and staff recognition are routinely scattered across unstructured mass emails and external chat channels.

Important notices get buried in crowded employee inboxes, leading to missed deadlines, compliance gaps, and zero visibility into whether staff members have actually seen and acknowledged critical notices.

Who Is This App For?

  • HR Managers & Officers: Need an audit-ready trail of policy distribution and employee confirmations.
  • Internal Communications Teams: Publishing structured, branded company bulletins.
  • Department Leaders: Sending targeted announcements exclusive to their engineering, sales, or support units.
  • Employees: Enjoying a clean, dedicated noticeboard with unread alerts so crucial updates are never missed.

Features in Detail

A closer look at how each component operates in daily HR workflows

Rich-Text Content & Type Classification

Model: hr.announcement • Views: Form, List, Kanban

Compose comprehensive announcements using Odoo rich-text HTML editor supporting headings, lists, bold formatting, links, and embedded tables. Classify notices into four distinct categories: General (informational), Warning (critical safety or policy alerts), Reward (employee achievements and bonuses), or Greeting (birthdays, work anniversaries, and holiday wishes).

  • Color-coded badges reflect the announcement type throughout list and kanban views.
  • Full chatter integration with <chatter/> allows team members to comment, ask questions, or log internal activities.

Target Audience Selection & Granular Scoping

Fields: target, department_ids, employee_ids

Tailor notice visibility precisely to who needs to see it. Choose between three broadcast scopes:

  • All Employees: Reaches every active employee across the company.
  • Departments: Select one or more departments via department_ids tag selection.
  • Specific Employees: Select individual staff members via employee_ids tags for targeted recognitions or discrete notices.

Automated Read-Receipts & Awareness Analytics

Model: hr.announcement.read • Stat Button: Read Awareness

Track policy acknowledgment effortlessly. When an employee reviews a published announcement, the system logs a permanent read receipt containing the employee profile and exact reading timestamp. A dedicated Read Awareness stat button displays the live ratio of read receipts versus the total targeted employee count (e.g. 18 / 20 read).

  • Dedicated “Read Receipts” notebook tab displays the full roster of staff who have read the announcement.
  • Visual Unread badges alert employees to pending announcements, while read notices appear cleanly muted.

Publishing Lifecycle & Auto-Expiry Cron

Statuses: Draft → Published → Expired • Action: Auto-Expire Announcements

Maintain a clean, organized noticeboard without manual cleanup. Prepare announcements in Draft, review with stakeholders, and click Publish when ready. When an optional expiry_date is reached, the automated daily scheduled action transitions the announcement to Expired, removing it from active notice filters.

  • Managers can manually expire or reset any announcement back to draft at any point.
  • Title uniqueness constraint per company prevents duplicate broadcasts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common technical and operational inquiries

Can standard employees edit or delete announcements?

No. Access rights are strictly separated via Odoo 18 security rules. Standard employees have read-only access to published announcements in their company and can only log their own read receipt. Full creation, editing, and deletion are restricted to HR Announcement Managers.

Does this module support multi-company setups?

Yes. Each announcement is tied to a company. Security rules ensure that employees only see announcements published by their active company or subsidiaries, preventing information leakage across corporate branches.

How do employees know an announcement has been published?

Upon clicking Publish, the system automatically posts an internal notification message to the Discuss inbox of every targeted employee. Additionally, unread notices are visually highlighted in the HR Announcements list and kanban views.

What happens if an announcement reaches its expiry date?

The built-in daily cron job scans for published announcements whose expiry date has passed and automatically transitions their status to Expired. Expired announcements remain archived in the database for auditing and historical review.

Can we uninstall the module cleanly without breaking Odoo?

Yes. The module depends only on standard Odoo Community modules (hr and mail) without modifying standard core tables. Uninstalling cleanly removes the custom models, views, and menu entries.

Is the module compatible with Odoo Enterprise?

Yes. The module is fully tested and 100% compatible with both Odoo 18.0 Community and Odoo 18.0 Enterprise environments.
