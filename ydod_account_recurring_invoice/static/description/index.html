How It Works in 4 Simple Steps

Start automating your recurring revenue in less than 2 minutes.

Create or Convert

Define a new recurring template or click "Create Recurrence" directly on any posted invoice.

2
Set Schedule & Rules

Choose interval (weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual), start date, and end criteria (forever, end date, or cycles).

3
Enable Automation

4
Monitor & Grow

The automated daily cron takes over. Inspect generated invoices and cumulative billed totals anytime.
