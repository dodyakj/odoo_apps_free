Purchase Vendor Minimum Order Guard

Enforces supplier-level minimum order values (MOV) and quantities (MOQ) on RFQs and POs. Eliminate supplier penalty fees, rejected purchase orders, and unnecessary freight surcharges.

Odoo 20.0 Ready MOV & MOQ Protection Manager Override

Key Features

Vendor-Level Thresholds

Configure Minimum Order Amount and Minimum Total Quantity directly on vendor contact records under the Purchase tab.

Live Warning Banner

A real-time status banner alerts buyers on RFQ and PO screens whenever current quantities or untaxed totals fall below supplier rules.

Confirmation Prevention

Blocks buyers from confirming non-compliant orders, displaying exact required values versus current order totals.

Manager Override

Purchase Managers with override privileges can confirm urgent procurements below minimums, leaving an automatic audit trail in the chatter.

Search & Grouping Filters

Instantly filter purchase orders with the "Below Vendor Minimums" search filter or group orders by compliance status.

List View Indicator

A clear boolean badge on the purchase order list view lets procurement planners quickly spot compliant orders at a glance.

How It Works

  1. Define Supplier Minimums
    Open any Vendor in Contacts or Purchase, navigate to the Sales & Purchase tab, and specify Minimum Order Amount and/or Minimum Order Quantity.
  2. Prepare Quotations or Purchase Orders
    As order lines are added, Odoo dynamically evaluates whether untaxed amounts and total units meet the vendor's policy. A prominent warning banner appears if thresholds are unmet.
  3. Automatic Guard on Confirmation
    Clicking "Confirm Order" validates thresholds. Non-compliant orders trigger a clear error message detailing shortfalls unless authorized by an override manager.
