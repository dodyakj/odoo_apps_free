Purchase Price Deviation Guard

Stop overpaying before the money is committed. Every purchase order line is compared with the last confirmed purchase price or the vendor pricelist. Lines above your tolerance are flagged in red and the order goes to To Approve until a Purchase Manager signs it off with a justification.

Catch typos, silent vendor price increases and inflated quotes, without building a full approval matrix.

See it in action

Price Deviations

Key Features

Smart reference price

Taken from the last confirmed purchase of the product, converted to the line unit of measure and currency, with the vendor pricelist as fallback.

Flexible tolerance

Company-wide percentage plus an optional minimum amount per unit, with overrides on volatile product categories such as raw materials or fuel.

Visible on every line

Reference price, deviation percentage and an "Above Tolerance" badge, with red highlighting directly in the order lines.

Standard approval flow

Hooks into Odoo's own approval check: deviating orders go to To Approve instead of being confirmed. No new states to learn.

Justified approvals

Managers approve through a dialog that requires a justification, logged in the chatter with the old and new prices.

Audit ready

"Price Deviation" filter and group-by on RFQs and purchase orders, plus a dedicated Price Deviations menu for controllers.

How to Use

1
Set your tolerance

Go to Purchase › Configuration › Settings and enable Price Deviation Check with a percentage and an optional amount.

2
Override categories

Open a product category and switch on Custom Price Tolerance for volatile goods. Sub-categories inherit it.

3
Buyers order as usual

Lines above tolerance turn red. Confirming such an order sends it to To Approve automatically.

4
Managers approve

Click Approve Price Deviation, review the lines, enter a justification and confirm. Everything is logged.
