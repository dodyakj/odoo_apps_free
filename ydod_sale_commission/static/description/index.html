Key Features

💰

Configurable Commission Plans

Define percentage-based commission plans scoped to a specific salesperson, a sales team, or a company-wide default that applies to everyone else.

⚡

Automatic Calculation

A commission line is generated as soon as a sale order is confirmed, based on its untaxed total and the most specific applicable plan.

📋

Full Audit Trail

Every line keeps its reference, salesperson, base amount, rate, commission and a lock/paid status, with a chatter history of every change.

📊

List & Pivot Reporting

Review total commissions per salesperson, team and period in list and pivot views, filterable by date range and paid status.

🔒

Lock & Manual Override

Adjust a draft commission whenever a correction is needed, then lock it to protect the amount before marking it as paid.

👤

Secure by Design

Salespeople only see their own commissions through a smart button on the order, while managers get full reporting and payout control.

How To Use

1

Go to Sales > Configuration > Commission Plans and create a plan with its rate.

2

Scope the plan to a salesperson, a sales team, or keep it as the company default.

3

Confirm a quotation as usual: the commission line is created automatically.

4

Review payouts in Sales > Reporting > Commissions, then lock, adjust and mark lines as paid.
