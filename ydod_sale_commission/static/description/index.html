Sales Commission Management

Stop tracking sales commissions in spreadsheets. Automatically calculate payouts for salespeople and teams the moment a quotation becomes a confirmed order.

Key Features

💰

Configurable Commission Plans

Define percentage-based commission plans scoped to a specific salesperson, a sales team, or a company-wide default that applies to everyone else.

⚡

Automatic Calculation

A commission line is generated automatically as soon as a sale order is confirmed, based on its untaxed total and the applicable plan rate.

📋

Full Audit Trail

Every commission line keeps its reference, salesperson, base amount, rate, computed commission and a lock/paid status, with a chatter history of every change.

📊

List & Pivot Reporting

Review total commissions per salesperson, team and period in dedicated list and pivot views, filterable by date range and paid status.

🔒

Lock & Manual Override

Lock a computed commission line to protect it, or manually adjust the amount whenever a correction is needed before marking it as paid.

👤

Smart Button on Orders

A commission smart button on the sale order gives salespeople and managers instant visibility of the commission generated by each order.

How To Use

1

Go to Sales > Configuration > Commission Plans and create a plan with its rate.

2

Scope the plan to a salesperson, a sales team, or leave it as the company default.

3

Confirm a quotation as usual: the commission line is created automatically.

4

Review payouts in Sales > Reporting > Commissions, lock, adjust and mark as paid.
