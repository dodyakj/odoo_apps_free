Customer Invoice Overdue Aging Summary & Dunning Alerts

Real-time customer aging buckets, credit limit enforcement, and automated daily dunning notifications to safeguard your cash flow.

Real-Time Aging Analysis

Instant breakdown of customer balances across Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ days directly on the partner profile.

Credit Limit Enforcement

Intelligent validation checks when confirming invoices to prevent high-risk customers from exceeding approved credit thresholds.

Automated Daily Alerts

Daily scheduled cron jobs notify assigned sales reps with detailed summaries of overdue customer accounts.

Risk Scoring Indicator

Categorize your clients into Low, Medium, and High risk profiles to tailor your collection strategies effectively.

How to Use

  1. Navigate to Invoicing > Customers > Customers and open any partner record.
  2. Inspect the Invoice Aging & Risk tab to review real-time aging buckets and risk scores.
  3. Configure credit limits and risk levels to automatically safeguard invoice validations.
  4. Relax as the automated daily cron job dispatches dunning summaries to your sales team.
