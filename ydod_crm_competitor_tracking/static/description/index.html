CRM Competitor Tracking & Win/Loss Analysis

Tag the competitors you are up against on every opportunity, let Odoo snapshot the outcome automatically, and finally know who you really win and lose against.

Key Features

🏷️

Competitor Directory

A lightweight Competitor model with name, website and free-form battle-card notes, so your team keeps strengths, weaknesses and talking points in one place.

🎯

Tag Competitors on Opportunities

A many2many "Competitors" tag field right on the opportunity form and kanban card, so reps record who they are up against in a couple of clicks.

⚡

Automatic Win/Loss Snapshot

The moment a tagged opportunity is marked Won or Lost, the result and the stage reached are captured automatically — no manual logging required.

📊

Win Rate Pivot & Graph

A ready-made Win/Loss Analysis report with pivot and graph views showing win rate, loss rate and total deal value per competitor.

🔗

Smart Button

Every competitor record has a one-click smart button listing every opportunity where that competitor was involved.

🧩

Built on Standard CRM

No extra configuration screens: it plugs straight into the existing Opportunity form, kanban and search views you already use.

How To Use

1

Go to CRM > Configuration > Competitors and create the competitors you regularly face, with their website and battle-card notes.

2

Open any opportunity and add one or more tags in the new Competitors field on the form, or directly from the kanban card.

3

Work the opportunity as usual. When you mark it Won or Lost, the result and the stage reached are saved automatically for every tagged competitor.

4

Open a Competitor record and click the Opportunities smart button, or go to CRM > Reporting > Competitor Win/Loss to see the pivot and graph report.
