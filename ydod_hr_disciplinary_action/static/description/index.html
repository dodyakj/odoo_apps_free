Employee Disciplinary Actions & Warning Management

Employee Disciplinary Actions & Warning Management

Track employee misconduct, formal warnings, and disciplinary history with a structured approval workflow and secure record keeping.

Odoo 18 Compatible Secure & Confidential

Key Features

Incident Categorization

Record disciplinary incidents with customizable categories ranging from Verbal Warnings to Termination and Severity levels.

Structured Workflow

Draft, review, and confirm formal disciplinary actions with controlled access status transitions and approval tracks.

Expiration Tracking

Automatically track warning expiration dates and manage employee rehabilitation periods over time.

How To Use

1
Record Incident

Go to HR > Disciplinary Actions > Create a new record selecting the employee, incident date, and severity.

2
Submit for Review

Document incident details, required corrective actions, and employee defense remarks before submission.

3
Confirm & Monitor

HR Managers confirm the warning to enforce active status and monitor expiration timelines.
